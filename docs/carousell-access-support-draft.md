## Exact message for review

**To:** help@thecarousell.com  
**Subject:** Philippines personal seller: supported buyer-message access and native replies

Hello Carousell Support,

I sell approximately 11–30 of my own secondhand items monthly in the Philippines. I am evaluating a private assistant on my Mac that could read selected sales conversations, prepare buyer replies for my review and, only if permitted, eventually send routine factual replies. No custom Carousell reader or sender has been connected.

Could you please clarify or route this to the appropriate team?

1. Is there an approved API or partner integration for reading and replying to my own buyer chats? Which account tier, permissions and written approval would be required? Please link the applicable documentation. If no supported option exists, please confirm that too.
2. For a personal Philippine seller, what is the lowest-cost current plan that includes native auto-replies? Is switching to a Professional account required, and would that introduce service fees or other recurring charges? Please distinguish web/iPhone monthly and annual pricing.
3. Can native replies be scoped per listing and scheduled, and are they only fixed acknowledgments or can they answer different buyer questions? How can I turn them off reliably before using another approved reply workflow?
4. If an approved integration exists, does it provide complete chat history, stable listing/conversation/message IDs, edits/deletions, replies I send in the native app, and a way to confirm remote delivery without duplicating a reply after a lost response?
5. Are chat-notification emails a supported input for a private assistant? Do they contain complete messages and stable identifiers, arrive immediately or as summaries, and support any approved reply path?

Please do not change my account or enroll me in a paid plan. I am asking about feasibility, permissions and costs first.

Thank you.
