5. **Stale approval:** approve a synthetic reply on one device, change its item's price/status on the other, then verify copying requires fresh review. Marking an inquiry Answered is a separate manual action; copying alone must leave it Open.

Only the participant's device evidence or an authorized live session can close these gates. Do not impersonate Janyl, confirm her email through an admin shortcut, or substitute a synthetic Auth row for her real acceptance.

## Channel release sequence remains outstanding

For each channel independently, record its permitted operations, account/region entitlement, trusted IDs, source completeness, edits/deletions, owner/native-reply visibility, and acknowledgment/reconciliation behavior. Unsupported operations stay unavailable. A login or an API's technical availability is not the access checkpoint.

Once a named adapter passes that gate:

1. Implement its owned capture/thread mapping and completeness tracking, with synthetic contract tests before a read-only pilot. Unknown item mappings and partial messages require review.
2. Run the read-only pilot for **at least seven days and 30 independently verified relevant events**; extend for low volume. Compare against the actual platform, target at least 95% capture and no wrong item mappings. Log missed messages and the owner's mapping effort.
3. After specific permission and owner authorization, review **at least 30 responses** in a supervised pilot. Record wrong recipients, factual corrections, public/private mistakes, duplicates and unknown outcomes. DM evidence does not validate Group comments.
4. Present measured coverage, owner minutes, latency, cost and limitations. Seek a specific routine-template release decision for a named channel. The targets of 70% routine handling and under-two-minute healthy-connector 95th-percentile latency are goals, not current results.
5. Verify pause acknowledgment and reconcile in-flight sends; review daily during the first released week. Do not extend to negotiations, reservations, payments or automatic outreach.
